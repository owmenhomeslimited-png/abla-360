Authorised fee adjustment

Choose an original fee charge, select whether to increase or reduce it, then enter the amount using numbers only. The system will reject a reduction that falls below the amount already paid.

Authorised payment correction

Select the original payment. If money was allocated to a fee, reverse the relevant allocation first. Then enter the amount being refunded, voided or corrected. Both actions are processed together.
